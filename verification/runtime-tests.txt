Known limits:
- These tests use a fake provider boundary. They do not establish live vendor outage recovery or live model execution.
- Automatic recovery requires explicit structured proof of a supported availability failure before assistant output or tool activity. Authentication, policy, cancellation, context, unknown, and post-work failures do not trigger replay.
- Recovery captures the original resolved input in runner memory. Missing capture after restart or unmatched terminal-only input is not automatically replayed.
- A replacement startup, history conversion, or unsettled original transport failure ends visibly; it does not blindly advance to another model.
- Persistent updates are atomic only when conversation data and metadata share the same engine/database. Split-database configurations decline before mutation.
- If two start acknowledgments remain unavailable, the coordinator attempts a token-scoped abort and exposes an explicit uncertain outcome. An unreachable runner cannot be claimed to have stopped, and no further automatic replay is attempted.
- The attempt budget is the primary model plus up to two configured backups; there is no additional same-model transient retry.
