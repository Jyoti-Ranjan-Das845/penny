# Writing Quality North Star

## Purpose

This guide is the shared quality contract for document planning, writing, and
review. It defines the outcome every role serves; it does not assign workflow
responsibilities, prescribe document structure, or grant permission to add,
correct, or invent content.

## North Star

> Help the intended reader understand the authorized meaning with no
> unnecessary effort.

A good document is faithful to what it must preserve, economical with the
reader's attention, coherent in how it unfolds, and deliberately crafted for
its purpose.

## The four dimensions

### Fidelity

The intended and authorized meaning survives.

- Preserve material facts, scope, certainty, qualifications, numbers, and
  attribution.
- Do not strengthen, weaken, generalize, or silently replace a claim.
- Do not present unsupported material as sourced or agreed.
- Keep evidence and the claim it supports meaningfully connected.

### Attention economy

Every element earns the attention it costs.

- Give each idea one primary home.
- Keep supporting detail only when it advances understanding, a required
  decision, or responsible interpretation.
- Remove repetition, throat-clearing, generic continuation, and decorative
  complexity.
- Use a table, list, diagram, or prose only when it makes the meaning easier
  to grasp than the alternatives.

### Coherence

The reader can follow the document without reconstructing its logic.

- Group information by its real relationship, not by superficial similarity.
- Make sequence, dependency, comparison, cause, and conclusion legible.
- Use stable terms for the same thing.
- Let the difficulty come from the subject, not from disordered presentation.

### Craft

The document feels deliberately composed for this purpose and reader.

- Prefer precise, natural language over stock phrases and template prose.
- Use rhythm, variation, emphasis, and transitions when they improve meaning.
- Choose a form that suits the content instead of applying conventions by
  habit.
- Make the document feel made, not mechanically assembled.

## Resolving tension

The dimensions reinforce each other, but they are not equal constraints.

1. Fidelity is non-negotiable.
2. Attention economy must not remove meaning, evidence, or qualification that
   fidelity requires.
3. Coherence organizes the necessary material so the reader can use it.
4. Craft improves the delivery of that material; it must not add unsupported
   substance or obscure the point.

In short: never improve economy, coherence, or craft by losing required
fidelity.

## Scope boundary

This guide is not an authority model. It does not decide whether a claim may be
added, corrected, inferred, or creatively invented. Those permissions belong
to the document's requirements and authoring contract. It is also not a
checklist: apply the dimensions where they materially affect the document.
