# Detailed design meeting — remote control prototype

**What was reviewed:** The clay prototype from the UI designer and industrial designer, scored by the team against the three agreed design criteria.

**The prototype as presented**
- Yellow (company colour) and red, built from a ball on a triangular base — the team called it the "mushroom" design, later reworked in the meeting to read more like a pineapple.
- Channel selection by turning the ball rather than pressing buttons; mushroom-shaped tactile buttons.
- Inside: low-level chip, LCD button, four-microphone array for voice commands, battery in the base, solar receptor on the outer surface.

**Scores (1 = meets the criterion, 7 = does not)**
- **Fancy look and feel — 3.** Agreed by all four. Colours were accepted, but the shape did not clearly read as the fruit-and-vegetable trend it was meant to follow, and there was too much red. The buttons were repositioned during the meeting to improve this.
- **Technologically innovative — 2.** Agreed. The embedded microphone array and the round, non-button form carried this.
- **Easy to use — not scored.** Raised as the last and most critical criterion; the meeting record ends before the team reached a number.

**Open items**
- Whether the solar receptor pushes up the unit cost. The industrial designer expects it will not; the project manager deferred this to the costing step.
- Price evaluation, and the go/no-go on production, both still to come.
- No decision taken on a product name; "mushroom" was only talk.
